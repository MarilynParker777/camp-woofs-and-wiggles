Services

Whether your pup is here for the day or staying overnight, we offer safe, supervised care with plenty of fun. Explore our main services below and contact us anytime with questions.

Day Camp

What is included:

  • Indoor and outdoor adventure areas
  • Nap time for full-day campers
  • Midday snack for full-day campers
  • Free medication administration
  • Individual attention from trained camp counselors

Full Day: $30 (1 dog)
Half Day: $20 (1 dog)
Add $10 per dog for full or half day

Boarding

What is included:

  • All the services we provide to our full-day campers
  • Private “bunks” for overnight campers with individual cameras for parent check-ins
  • Free bath and nail trim for guests staying 3+ days

Nightly rate: $40 (1 dog)
Add $15 per dog, per day in a shared bunk (max 3)

Note: All campers must be vaccinated, spayed/neutered, and pass the free temperament assessment (free 1/2 day boarding session).

Grooming & Baths

Keep your pup fresh and comfortable with add-on grooming services during day camp or boarding. We offer gentle bathing, brushing, and basic clean-up options so your dog goes home happy and clean.

Want something specific? Contact us and we will help you choose the best option for your dog's coat and needs.

Training

Training sessions are designed to support good behavior and confidence using positive, consistent techniques. Whether your dog needs help with basic manners or learning new skills, we will create a plan that fits your goals.

Ask us about training add-ons during camp or boarding for extra practice and structure.

Pup Parties

Celebrate your pup's big day with a fun, supervised party! Pup parties can include playtime, treats, and photo-worthy moments for your dog and their friends.
