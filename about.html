About

Mission Statement

At Camp Woofs and Wiggles, our mission is to provide your beloved canine companions with a safe, fun, and enriching environment—whether it is a day of play or an extended stay. Our dedicated team ensures your dog experiences love, adventure, and personalized care. We offer services beyond traditional boarding, including training, grooming, and pup parties, because every dog deserves a wag-worthy experience.

Our Story

Camp Woofs and Wiggles was created for dog parents who want more than a basic drop-off kennel. We built a place where dogs can play, rest, and feel comfortable—like a second home. Located in Lincoln, Nebraska, our space is designed for safe indoor and outdoor enrichment, with staff who treat every pup like family.

From full-day camp to overnight boarding, our goal is simple: a clean, supervised, and joyful experience for every dog—plus peace of mind for every owner.

What We Believe

  • Safety first: supervision, clean spaces, and clear routines
  • Enrichment matters: play, movement, and mental stimulation
  • Kind care: gentle handling, patience, and positive support
  • Communication: we are here to help you feel confident and informed
